Who I am

About

I am a Master of Engineering graduate from Saga University, Japan (GPA 3.9/4.0), specialising in RFID-based Digital Twin simulation for emergency department resource management, under Professor Eisuke Hanada.

What drives my work is a practical problem: Bangladesh's public hospitals operate at 3–4× capacity, yet deploying RFID without prior evidence is expensive and risky. I build simulation tools that generate that evidence cheaply — before any hardware is purchased. My thesis was defended on July 14, 2026, with all three research questions fully supported by experimental data.

I am actively seeking a fully funded PhD position in healthcare informatics, biomedical engineering, or IoT systems — primarily targeting Australia (RTP) and international programs.
